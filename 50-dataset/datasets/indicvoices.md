# [[indicvoices]] — AI4Bharat

Large crowdsourced Indic speech corpus: read + spontaneous speech across the scheduled languages; spontaneous segments are the natural habitat of code-mixing and disfluencies.

- **Use for:** primary natural-speech source — spontaneous samples for `code_mixed`, `fast_speech`, `interruption` conditions; speaker diversity.
- **Why priority 1:** same ecosystem as the model ([[indic-transcribe-core]]) → realistic domain match, and its recording conditions (phone/mic uploads) naturally span audio conditions.
- **Caveats:** reference verification quality varies by contributor — spot-check before trusting; ⚠️ to-verify: per-language hour counts and whether bho/mai/ur coverage suffices for our 8-language set (bho especially).
