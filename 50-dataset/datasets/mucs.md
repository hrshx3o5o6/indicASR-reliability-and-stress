# [[mucs]] — Multilingual & Code-switch corpus

Code-mixed/mirrored Indic speech collections (Telugu, Tamil, Hindi focus) from the IMaD challenge era.

- **Use for:** the `code_mixed` / `heavy_code_mixed` conditions — naturalistic English-in-Indic mixing, which read corpora lack.
- **Caveats:** ⚠️ to-verify current availability + exact language coverage; references may be code-mix-normalized in ways that interact with [[oi-wer]] scoring — check the normalization convention before using.
- Covers hi + ta + te of our set; ml/kn code-mixing may need IndicVoices mining instead.
