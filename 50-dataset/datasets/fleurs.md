# [[fleurs]] — Google

n-way **parallel** speech corpus (102 languages): same sentences recorded across languages.

- **Use for:** *controlled cross-language comparison* — near-identical content across ta/hi/te/ml/kn/ur ⇒ language-diff OI-WER is apples-to-apples, ideal for the language-stratified tables of [[EXP-A]].

- Role is surgical: the cross-language equal-content slice of the suite, not the whole suite.
