## Tier 0 — sanity-checked existing claims
- **F-003 (EXP-E) retracted.** `run_exp_e.py`'s `transcribe_clip()` called
  `asr.transcribe(...)[0]` without `return_lid=True` — `transcribe()` returns
  a bare `str` in that case, so `[0]` indexed the first *character*, not a
  tuple element. Confirmed directly on the per-sample records (`"य उ"` for a
  60 s two-chunk clip; oracle-turn token counts matched segment counts
  exactly in all 4 recordings, independent of segment duration). The
  published "vendor's diarize-then-transcribe remedy is insufficient even
  with oracle boundaries" claim (WER 99.8–100%) was this bug, not a model
  result. Fixed in code; DER (62.51%, unaffected by the bug) survives as a
  directional signal; WER numbers are void until rerun with a live ASR
  service. This is the single highest-value finding of the session — it
  would have been the report's most dramatic (and wrong) number.
- **EXP-D annotation-count "discrepancy" (P-11) reconciled — not a bug.**
  `annotated_rows_scored: 50` counts rows with *any* annotated slot; "11
  number rows" is the has_numbers subset. Verified from the row list: 11
  number, 3 date, 3 currency, 39 entity rows. Practical effect: the headline
  currency 100%→0% collapse (F-004/C2) rests on n=3, not n=50 — narrowed
  confidence accordingly, kept the entity claim (n=39) at medium-high.
